Why we started this

A panicked industry. Overbearing AI hype. And no roadmap for the next generation of finance leaders.

🫣

As Startup CFOs and finance leaders, we've scaled teams, made high-stakes calls, and led through chaos

But over the past few years, we started seeing the same patterns emerge:

  • Smart, ambitious finance professionals stuck in the weeds - with no clear path to strategic work.
  • Colleagues panicking, and chasing credentials or shiny new AI tools, while neglecting the strategic and commercial skills that really move the needle.
  • A flood of AI hype from LinkedIn-fluencers, but no real answers to the questions we were hearing weekly:
  • “What should I focus on?”
  • “What does good look like now?”
  • “Where am I falling behind?”

We could see finance becoming a tale of two paths:

↙ ↘
1

A small group of strategic thinkers rising above the automation layer

2

Everyone else drowning in technical AI noise and uncertainty.

Yes, AI will automate workflows and jobs might disappear faster than people can pivot.

But surely the answer wasn't to complete another course on the latest AI prompts? There had to be a better way to get through this.

🎯 What you actually need

Your edge is human.

The last defensible frontier in finance isn't another prompt library or technical tool - it's durable, human skills.

In a world racing toward automation, the real opportunity is staying human. Blending AI-native thinking with taste, commercial & strategic instinct, and nuanced decision-making power will help you get sharper.

So who are we?

Since our early days in corporate finance, investment banking and getting Big 4-qualified, we've spent the last decade inside startups - as Founders, Finance Leads and CFOs.

We've experienced the brutal finance grind from every angle:

  • From scrappy bootstrapped startups to multi-billion-dollar corporates and fundraises from the worlds top investors
  • From setting up finance functions from scratch to scaling teams across continents
  • From wild success stories and flashy M&A deals… to navigating full-blown 'oh-shit' moments, restructures and layoffs.

And before all that?

We were just some job-hopping millennials - buried in Frankenstein Excel models, juggling half-built systems and dashboards, trying to keep seniors happy ... whilst still sleeping at night.

We've learned what works, what doesn't, and how clarity, commercial instinct and confidence can transform a career.

Now, as we navigate the new AI era - for ourselves and our teams - we're here to share those hard-won lessons, cut through the noise, and give you the kind of career momentum that AI can't.
