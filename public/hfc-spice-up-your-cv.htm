Why we made this guide

We’ve seen too many high-potential finance professionals get overlooked - not for lack of skill, but because their CV doesn’t signal what they’re truly capable of.

They list technical finance tasks (not impact), and get lost in a sea of sameness.

Common mistakes:

  • ❌
    "Responsible for month-end" ≠ commercial operator
  • ❌
    Recruiters skim-read, and you’re not making it easy to stand out
  • ❌
    You’re doing (some) strategic work, but your CV still reads like a technical accountant’s

In a world where AI is reshaping workflows, it's your human edge - and how you communicate it - that really cuts through.

Overlooking this often means missing out on interviews for those high-impact roles you deserve.
