FAQs

Is this a good fit if I’m not job-hunting?

Yes - many clients come to sharpen their presence in their current roles, nail a promotion, gain exposure to wider projects or clarify their next step.

Can I send my CV / job description / topics to discuss ahead of time?

Absolutely - we encourage it. You'll get a details of where to send once you book. We provide completely personalised guidance for every client.

What if I want more support after the call?

You’ll have the option to continue with a coaching package, book more blocks of 30-minutes or just take the insights and run. No pressure. You can also speak to peers and experts in the HFC community.

Will you write my CV / Resume for me from scratch?

No - and that's a good thing. We don't believe in shortcuts or templates. Instead, we'll given you pointed, high-impact feedback and detailed human edits to help you do it better yourself. You'll come away with a CV that reflects your unique edge - not a generic clone.

How long after purchasing one of the coaching packages will the services end?

The package will be valid for 2 months after purchase. Once you’ve kicked off usage of the package, you have 14 days to complete the coaching package before it expires.

How many emails and questions can I ask once I have purchased a coaching package?

Within each package there are a limited number of document and email edits, depending on which package you purchase (details listed within each package). The reason we do this is that each version edit takes a considerable amount of human time as we endeavor to truly understand your background, experiences and what a given job application requires before even commencing our work. For this reason we encourage our customers to work with us from the outset to get the documents to the high level of quality they need to be in as few versions as possible.

We will of course respond to a reasonable number of questions in order to provide you with a quality service that reflects the purchased package requirements. Note that the Essential and Pro packages will benefit from priority response.

We will not answer queries that are not part of the package you have purchased, but we can upgrade you to the package that does cover the context you need if you wish us to do so (or a 30-min call, if that's more appropriate to your needs).

How long will you take to respond to my questions & messages?

We generally respond within 1 business day and on many occasions sooner than this.
